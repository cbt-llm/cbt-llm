Where do LLMs fall short in CBT‑guided reasoning?

LLMs know Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), but lack clinical structure for mental health-related queries. We introduce CBT Lens, a clinically grounded framework with a novel metric to evaluate whether it changes a model’s baseline behavior.

04 / Protocol Leverage Force

Did our framework change the model’s baseline behavior?

Protocol Leverage Force (F) measures how strongly our framework reorients a response away from the model’s baseline behavior and toward the user’s utterance. Models often favored Validation & Reflection, resulting in F = 1.18%–1.34% across three open-weight models. A low F means guidance barely moves the model off its default reply, so responses stay lexically close to the baseline.

05 / The affective story

What do valence and arousal show us?

Valence describes how positive or negative an emotion feels. Arousal describes its level of physiological activation or energy. These are the two dimensions of Russell’s Circumplex Model of Affect ↗. We compare user language in our synthetic sessions with RealCBT human transcripts to trace how emotion shifts over the course of a conversation.
